Lagrange points

For some reason, there is minimum word requirement on this assigmnent, so i will proceed to talk about random stuff here. First up is Lagrange points, from how i understand them

What are Lagrange points?

Lagrange points are points in space where there is a perfect balance of either two bodies' gravitational pull or a balance between one body's gravitational pull and the centrifugal force of a larger orbit than the body's.
Oversimplified and explained poorly, but i am neither an astrophysicist nor a teacher.

Lagrange points of gravitational balance

For this example we will use the Earth system. Earth has two gravitational lagrange points, one with the moon and one with the sun, called L1. This lagrange point is the point between the Sun/Moon and the Earth where their gravities cancel out. This point is hard to stay in and not really that useful
